## The Core Loop

> [!summary] Hunt Loop
> **Accept Contract → Research → Prepare → Track → Encounter → Break Parts → Slay or Capture → Harvest → Craft → Upgrade → Increase Reputation**

## Three Principles

### 1. Information is power
Players who investigate a quarry should gain concrete benefits: resistances, vulnerabilities, behaviors, lairs, breakable parts, or preparation options.

### 2. How you kill it matters
A smashed horn, severed tail, ruined venom sac, or burnt hide should affect what can be harvested.

### 3. Monsters are the treasure
The most memorable equipment should come from creatures the party personally hunted.
