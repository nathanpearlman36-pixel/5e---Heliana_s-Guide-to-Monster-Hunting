# Tracking and Research

This system makes locating a quarry an active part of the hunt instead of a single Survival check.

## Monster Insights

Each successful research action, field discovery, or examination can reveal one useful **Monster Insight**.

Choose or roll:

| d10 | Monster Insight |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Damage resistance or immunity |
| 2 | Damage vulnerability or particularly effective damage type |
| 3 | Strong saving throw or condition resistance |
| 4 | Weak saving throw |
| 5 | Signature attack or recharge ability |
| 6 | Breakable body part and what happens if it breaks |
| 7 | Preferred terrain, nest, or migration route |
| 8 | Feeding behavior, bait, or lure |
| 9 | Enrage, retreat, or defensive behavior |
| 10 | Valuable organ/material and how to preserve it |

The DM should reveal **actionable information**, not vague lore.

Bad insight: "It dislikes cold."

Good insight: "Its throat sac stiffens in cold conditions. Cold damage prevents its flame breath from recharging until the end of its next turn."

## Research Checks

A character can research before departure if an appropriate source is available.

Suggested DC:

| Quarry Rarity | DC |
|---|---:|
| Common local monster | 10 |
| Uncommon predator | 13 |
| Rare apex creature | 16 |
| Legendary/Elder monster | 19 |
| Nearly unknown creature | 22+ |

### Degrees of Success

- **Fail by 5 or more:** no insight; the source may also be misleading or incomplete.
- **Failure:** no insight.
- **Success:** learn 1 Monster Insight.
- **Success by 5+:** learn 2 related Monster Insights.
- **Natural 20:** learn 2 insights and gain advantage on the first relevant field check using that information.

## Tracking Scenes

A hunt is divided into tracking scenes. In each scene, one character is the **Lead Tracker** and other characters may support.

### Lead Tracking Check

Use Survival by default. Other skills can apply if the fiction supports them:

- Investigation for damaged objects, blood spatter, or disturbed structures
- Nature for feeding signs and animal behavior
- Perception for distant movement, calls, or scent
- Arcana for magical residue

Suggested Tracking DC:

| Condition | DC Modifier |
|---|---:|
| Fresh obvious trail | -3 |
| Normal trail | +0 |
| Rain, wind, heavy traffic | +2 |
| Rocky/urban terrain | +3 |
| Monster deliberately conceals trail | +4 |
| Monster wounded below half HP | advantage |
| Party has matching bait, scent, map, or research clue | advantage |

## Trail Points

A successful tracking scene earns **1 Trail Point**. Exceptional evidence can earn 2.

Typical thresholds:

- 3 — simple/local hunt
- 5 — standard hunt
- 7 — difficult hunt
- 9 — legendary expedition

At the threshold, the party reaches the quarry, lair, or a decisive encounter.

## Supporting the Tracker

Before the Lead Tracker rolls, up to two other characters may attempt a support action.

Examples:

- scout ahead with Perception
- inspect droppings with Nature
- identify claw marks with Medicine
- climb for a better view with Athletics
- question survivors with Persuasion

On a successful DC 12 support check, the Lead Tracker gains **+1** to the check. If both supports succeed, the Lead Tracker gains **+2** instead.

A natural 20 on support grants advantage instead of +1.

## Failed Tracking Checks

Failure should move the hunt forward with a cost rather than stop play.

On a failure, choose one consequence:

- lose time
- consume supplies
- attract a minor monster
- trigger a hazard
- enter the wrong zone
- quarry gains distance
- quarry becomes aware of the hunters
- one character gains a level of exhaustion after prolonged travel, if appropriate

A failure normally earns **no Trail Point**, but the story still advances.

## Field Signs

Useful signs include:

- footprints
- shed scales or fur
- claw marks
- scat
- partially eaten prey
- territorial markings
- broken trees
- nests
- elemental residue
- unusual weather
- frightened wildlife
- discarded quills, teeth, or shell fragments
- blood
- molt or skin

A field sign can serve three purposes at once:

1. earn a Trail Point,
2. foreshadow a monster ability,
3. provide a Monster Insight.

## Quarry Awareness

For tense hunts, track **Awareness** from 0 to 3.

- **0 — Unaware:** hunters may gain surprise or favorable positioning.
- **1 — Suspicious:** quarry patrols and watches its territory.
- **2 — Alert:** quarry prepares an ambush or relocates.
- **3 — Hunting the Hunters:** quarry actively stalks the party.

Increase Awareness when the party:

- fails tracking loudly
- fights near the lair
- uses explosives or obvious magic
- leaves survivors among allied monsters
- triggers alarms or territorial signs

Decrease Awareness with camouflage, stealth, false trails, or waiting long enough for the quarry to relax.

## Bait and Lures

A successful research check may reveal suitable bait.

Using proper bait can:

- grant advantage on one tracking check
- reduce Trail Points needed by 1
- move the monster into favorable terrain
- delay its escape

Powerful apex monsters may recognize crude traps; clever preparation should be rewarded.

## Environmental Complications

Roll or choose once or twice per hunt:

| d8 | Complication |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Sudden storm or temperature shift |
| 2 | Rival predator enters the territory |
| 3 | Trail crosses dangerous terrain |
| 4 | Minor monsters swarm the area |
| 5 | Target doubles back |
| 6 | Injured survivor needs help |
| 7 | Valuable resource node distracts the party |
| 8 | The quarry is already fighting something else |

## Research Payoff Rule

Whenever a player correctly uses an Insight the party discovered earlier, reward it mechanically. Good options:

- advantage on one attack or check
- +2 to a relevant save DC
- suppress a monster feature for one round
- reduce damage by 1 die
- make a part easier to break

The point of research is to make preparation feel worth doing.
