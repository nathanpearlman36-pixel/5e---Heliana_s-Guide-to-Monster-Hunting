# DM Quick Reference

Use this page during play.

## Hunt Loop

**Contract → Research → Prepare → Track → Encounter → Break Parts → Slay/Capture → Harvest → Craft**

## Research DCs

| Quarry | DC |
|---|---:|
| Common | 10 |
| Uncommon | 13 |
| Rare/Apex | 16 |
| Legendary | 19 |
| Nearly unknown | 22+ |

**Success:** 1 Monster Insight  
**Success by 5+:** 2 Insights

## Monster Insights

Reveal one:

- resistance/immunity
- vulnerability/effective damage
- strong save/condition defense
- weak save
- signature attack
- breakable part
- habitat/lair
- bait/feeding behavior
- enrage/retreat behavior
- valuable material/preservation method

## Trail Points

| Hunt | Points Needed |
|---|---:|
| Simple | 3 |
| Standard | 5 |
| Difficult | 7 |
| Legendary | 9 |

**Successful tracking scene:** +1 point.  
**Major clue:** +2 possible.  
**Failure:** complication; story still advances.

## Tracking Modifiers

- fresh obvious trail: DC -3
- bad weather: DC +2
- rocky/urban ground: DC +3
- monster conceals trail: DC +4
- wounded monster below half HP: advantage
- useful research/bait/map: advantage

## Part Targeting

| Part | AC Modifier |
|---|---:|
| Huge/easy | +0 |
| Normal | +2 |
| Small/mobile | +4 |
| Tiny/precise | +6 |

If attack misses only because of part AC modifier, it still hits monster normally but deals **no Part Damage**.

## Break Thresholds

| Durability | Threshold |
|---|---:|
| Fragile | 10% max HP |
| Standard | 15% |
| Tough | 20% |
| Extreme | 25% |

Effective damage: **1.5× Part Damage**  
Ineffective damage: **½ Part Damage**

## Common Break Effects

- wing → reduce flight
- leg → reduce speed
- horn → weaken magical feature
- jaw → weaken bite
- armor → AC -1 or -2
- elemental organ → weaken recharge/signature attack
- tail → remove tail attack

## Severing

A severable part is severed when:

1. its threshold is reached, and
2. at least half its accumulated Part Damage was slashing.

| Material | DC |
|---|---:|
| Tough/obvious | 10 |

| Delicate | 16 |

Modifiers:

- clean part break: -2
- ideal specialized tool: -2

- destructive damage: +2 to +5
- known preservation method: advantage

## Harvest Results

**Success:** gain material.  
**Success by 5+:** +1 unit, better quality, or auto-preserve.  
**Failure:** attempt spent.  
**Fail by 5+:** specific extraction ruined.  
**Natural 20:** material + Rare Find.

## Spoilage

- bone/horn/scale/shell: stable
- hide/meat/blood: ~1 hour
- gland/eye/sac: ~30 minutes
- volatile magical organ: ~10 minutes

After safe window: disadvantage.  
After twice safe window: usually unusable without magic.

## Capturing

Monster is normally Capturable when:

- at or below 25% max HP,
- restrained/unconscious/incapacitated/trapped,
- hunter applies sedative or capture gear.

Target makes Constitution save against:

**8 + hunter proficiency bonus + hunter Intelligence or Wisdom modifier**

Failure = captured.  
Success = resists and cannot be retried until end of its next turn.

## DM Rule of Thumb

Reward players for:

- researching correctly,
- preparing intelligently,
- targeting parts deliberately,
- preserving materials carefully,
- hunting specific monsters for specific recipes.

The whole system works best when **knowledge changes the fight and the fight changes the loot**.
